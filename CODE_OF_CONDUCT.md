# Community code of conduct

Keep collaboration respectful, constructive and focused on the project. Welcome
people with different backgrounds and levels of technical experience. Critique
ideas and code, and assume good faith when asking questions or giving feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, spam and publication of
another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post utility
bills, account details, precise addresses, access tokens or private diagnostics.

For harassment, threats or other violations of GitHub's community guidelines,
use [GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not open a public issue containing private reports or personal information.
Report software vulnerabilities through the private channel in SECURITY.md.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation
using the repository's moderation tools and GitHub's policies. This project is
maintained by volunteers; no response time or support contract is promised.
